# Fundamentals — how to build a body

Measured doctrine from the reference bodies. (No invented Hz; frequencies come from
doc 04 rails and doc 05 tables.)

## Start from a six-stage actor program

Not six arbitrary EQ peaks — six **actors**, each with a job (anchor, mover, cut,
cap...). Register the jobs across the four corners, then let each actor's pole and
zero move as the body needs.

## Every active stage gets a zero — two roles

Zeros are mandatory (doc 01). The references use **two zero roles**, measured:

- **Local zero** — within one octave of its pole: a peak + canyon cavity. **58.5%** of endpoint zeros.
- **Remote zero** — more than 1.5 octaves from its pole: broad tilt, air cap, low-body
  excavation, cross-spectrum tension. **28.2%**.

A body that only hugs (local) or only counterweights (remote) is missing half the
vocabulary. Strong references combine both.

## The base shape (the HOME / M0 foundation)

- **Cliff** — loud low, steep falloff above. Dark.
- **Shelf** — quiet low, rising toward the top. Bright.
- **Window** — pole and zero move in opposite directions, opening/closing a gap.
- **Vocal mountains** — several peaks on the formant rails.
- **Sweep field** — all stages slide together (a length / cutoff sweep).

## Motion posture is a choice, and it's measurable

How much a body MOVES is a property you pick. Measured as Morph RMS (how far it
travels) and Secondary RMS (how hard Push works):

- **Big Morph movers**: ace_of_bass, razor_blades, meaty_gizmo (Morph RMS ~34-46 dB).
- **Modest movers**: talking_hedz, dj_alkaline (~15-20 dB).
- **Hard Push**: lucifer_s_q, meaty_gizmo, millennium (Secondary RMS ~34-41 dB).
- **Light Push**: talking_hedz, dj_alkaline (~6-8 dB).

Decide up front whether the body is a big traveler or a held frame — timid movement
is what separates a forgettable body from a premium one.

## What premium looks like (measured)

1-4 notches + wide Morph travel + Push tightens + max pole radius ~0.999 (edge but
stable). Across a PACK, spread the tilt: some dark, some bright, some flat.
