# Placement by intent — where the poles and zeros go

The core of the pack. Pick a body type; this says where the six poles and their
zeros sit, in Hz, and how they move. **Every frequency is measured (the rails,
doc 04) or published (the tables, doc 05) — never invented.** Snap to a real
frequency; never place at a random Hz.

## Vowel body

- **Poles = the formant rails.** Stage 1 (throat / F1) sits **199-580 Hz** (measured
  across 5 vocal references). F2 / F3 / F4 = the vowel formants in doc 05. The most
  used measured vowel rails are F1 at 270 / 390 / 440 / 530 / 540 / 640 Hz (doc 04).
- **Zeros = a deep canyon per formant.** Each formant carries one near-unit-circle
  notch beside it (a local zero, doc 02), plus optionally a nasal/tear remote zero.
- **Motion = SLIDE.** Formants glide between vowels; they do NOT cross rank (in
  oo->ee, F2 climbs 870 -> 2290 Hz but stays below F3 the whole way). Morph slides
  the vowel; Push tightens Q without moving the frequency.

## Tube body

- **Poles = the harmonic partial rails** (doc 05). Open-open = full harmonic series
  (e.g. 50 cm: 343 / 686 / 1029 / 1372 ... Hz). Closed-open = odd harmonics only.
  Measured rails confirm both land (doc 04, tube section, open/closed sub-groups).
- **Zeros** between partials to deepen the hollow.
- **Motion = LENGTH.** Shorten the tube and the WHOLE series scales up in lock-step
  (the variant ladder in doc 01 is exactly this — ratios 1.0 / 0.92 / 0.46 / 0.23).

## Metal body

- **Poles = inharmonic modes x a fundamental** (doc 05). Not integer multiples —
  the inharmonicity is the metal. E.g. free bar @440: 440 / 1213 / 2378 / 3931 Hz.
- **Zeros** between the dense modes for shimmer.
- **Motion = PLAY POSITION.** The mode frequencies stay FIXED; the morph shifts
  GAIN emphasis across them, it does not move them.

## Aggressive / violent body

- **Wide Morph travel** (big mover, doc 02) — relocate poles a lot between corners.
- **Remote zeros as kill rows** — the >1.5-octave-from-pole zero (28.2% of the
  corpus, doc 02). This is "remote-cut violence": notches parked far from their
  peaks, sweeping as kills.
- **High-bank collapse** — high stages fall DOWN into the mid as Morph advances
  instead of opening up (the most common move, 25 of 50 skins).
- **Edge-stable radius ~0.999** — sit poles at the brink for the scream, but stable.
- Frequencies: spread the poles across the measured rails (doc 04); park the zeros
  remote. The aggression is the notch moving away from the peak, not louder gain.
