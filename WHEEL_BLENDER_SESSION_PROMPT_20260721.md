## What the object actually is (this took a month to learn — do not re-derive)

- It is a **saucer**, not a barrel, not a drum, not a plain cylinder. A convex
  disc that **protrudes** slightly toward the viewer. The word "barrel" is the
  failure state: if the render has a strong dark→light→dark vertical cylinder
  gradient and bulging log-like end caps, it is wrong.
- Each tooth is a **paired top fin + bottom fin with a dark middle channel**
  between them. The channel has **slot windows**.
- The glow is a **backlight shining through the middle-channel slots** — it sits
  *inset* in the channel, and the dark fins cross *in front* of it. The cyan
  must never look like it sits on top of / protrudes from the teeth.
- The teeth are **angled/diagonal paddles**; the feel is a detented
  "click-click" wheel.
- It rotates so the teeth scroll **left→right** (value 0 at left, 100 at right).
  Never a vertical scrolling drum.
