# P2K Family Reverse-Engineering Notes

> **Clean-room: study only.** No coefficients/bytes/names shipped. Per-preset numbers are
> MEASURED from `ref/presets/*.bin` through the shipped `trench_core` engine; family taxonomy and
> aggregate bounds come from `dev/tmp/x3_p2k_family_browser/` contracts. Generated by `tools/p2k_family_notes.py`.

## Finding 1 — two different FORMATS

- **Designer `P2k_000–032`** = packed-morph 4-corner bodies. They render through `trench_core`
  (measured below). This is the *Morph Designer* format — the same one the forge authors.
- **Functional `P2k_033–049`** = **fixed-class parametric blocks** (2/4/6-pole LP/HP/BP, swept EQ,
  phaser, flanger, vowel). Through the packed engine they read **silent (−180 dB) or overflow (+227 dB)**
  — they are NOT packed-morph bodies. (0/17 rendered.) Their metrics
  below come from the X3 reverse-engineering, not this engine.

## Finding 2 — iconic = MOTION (the number)

The functional **noun** types and the designer **verb** presets are the **same 5 families**. The
difference is **motion**, and it is large *(source: x3_p2k_family_browser contracts (validated; functional baseline from X3 analysis since the functional .bin do not render here))*:

- median **morph-motion**: functional **6.49 dB RMS** → designer **17.59 dB RMS**
- median **Q-pressure**: functional **4.82 dB RMS** → designer **10.56 dB RMS**
- *(corroboration — the 33 packed designer .bin measured here through `trench_core`: morph-motion median **25.97 dB**, Q-pressure **16.88 dB**)*

**Iconic = same family, the actor travels far harder and Q exposes notches harder.** A behavioral
target as a number — not a copied coefficient.

- **intent:** moving peak/canyon actors and contrary band windows
- **contract bias:** morph owns actor motion; Q tightens and exposes notches
- **functional nouns (X3 menu):** 2 Pole Bandpass, 4 Pole Bandpass, Contrary Bandpass, Swept EQ 1 Octave, Swept EQ 2/1 Octave, Swept EQ 3/1 Octave

### Vocal / Formant  (`vocal_formant`)
- **intent:** formant ladder, mouth motion, and anti-formant canyons
