### 2. Verdict

The first Forge should be a **foundation-first, Morph-Designer-like program composer that writes genuine four-corner ROM-class bodies**, with response plots as the hero and six indexed row lanes as visible underlay. It is **not** a clone of historical Morph Designer, because Secondary must not be duplicated by default. It is **not** a full six-row four-corner root-domain editor, because that invites blank-canvas coefficient wandering before the author has a structural idea. The right first move is: deterministic lawful constructors ? packed-runtime four-corner body ? contact-sheet judgment ? row-aware macro repair by the human author.

---

### 3. The Smallest Honest Workflow

1. Pick one lawful foundation recipe: cliff, rising edge, opposed window, or simple cavity cluster.  
2. Set a few structural macros: anchor range, depth/height, width/slope, Morph travel, Secondary stress, optional remote counterweight amount.  
3. Compile directly into the locked four-corner packed bank: `M0_S0`, `M1_S0`, `M0_S1`, `M1_S1`, six rows each.  
4. Inspect plots generated through the real packed-domain interpolation path: corners, center, Morph strip, Secondary strip, and held-out off-axis states.  
5. Inspect the six row lanes only as correspondence lanes: contribution, numerator/denominator behavior, zeros, poles, warnings.  
6. Make one deliberate repair: adjust a macro, freeze a lane, change a lane’s structural role, or remove a counterweight.  
7. Only after the plotted surface tells a coherent story, audition briefly; the author keeps or kills it, not the machine.

### 4. Strategic Opportunities

1. **Four-corner expansion of compact program authoring.**  
It preserves Morph Designer speed while escaping its effectively one-dimensional Secondary duplication.  
Test it by building three recipes where Morph and Secondary affect different parameters, then require the center and two off-axis packed states to remain musically intentional on plots.

2. **Response-led, row-aware judgment.**  
It gives the visual author a fast top-level curve while preserving the six indexed lanes that actually carry structure.  
Test it by asking the author to diagnose one bad generated body using only full-response plots plus lane underlays, then perform a named macro repair within ten minutes.

3. **Remote-zero counterweight as a first-class lawful ingredient.**  
Remote zeros are a major source of broad tension, air caps, slopes, and excavation; hiding them will make the Forge timid.  
Test it by adding one counterweight macro to cliff/window recipes and comparing before/after packed contact sheets for intentional broad motion rather than local notch clutter.

4. **Packed-middle quality gate.**  
Endpoint beauty is cheap; bad bodies reveal themselves at the bilinear middle and off-axis held states.  
Test it by sampling a fixed `5 x 5` Morph/Secondary grid for every candidate and flagging unstable poles, excessive gain jumps, center collapse, and sub-200 collisions as warnings.

5. **Recordable plot-first authorship.**  
The Forge can double as short-form content because the creative act is visible: lawful constructor, plotted tension, human repair, ear check.  
Test it by recording one 30-second capture from recipe selection to plot rejection/keep decision with no vendor data, no AI score, and no hidden selection.

### 5. First Build

Build exactly one slice: **Foundation Composer v0: three deterministic four-corner constructors with row-aware plots.**

**Visible controls**

- `Recipe`: `Dark Cliff`, `Rising Edge`, `Opposed Window`.  
- `Anchor`: one frequency-region control, expressed musically/logarithmically.  
- `Intensity`: depth/height of the main structure.  
- `Width/Slope`: narrow-to-broad structural spread.  
- `Morph Travel`: how strongly the structure changes from `M0` to `M1`.  
- `Secondary Stress`: how `S1` differs from `S0`; never default to duplication unless explicitly chosen.  
- `Remote Counterweight`: off / low / high, using deterministic lawful placement rules.  
- Six lane chips: show active/inactive, structural role, solo/mute for plotting only, and freeze/unfreeze; no arbitrary coefficient typing.

**Plot outputs**

- Full cascade magnitude response for all four corners.  
- Packed-runtime center response at `M=0.5, S=0.5`.  
- Morph contact strip and Secondary contact strip generated through the actual packed interpolation path.  
- Fixed held-out grid: at least `5 x 5` sampled states, shown as compact heat/contact view.  
- Six row-lane underlays showing each lane’s contribution and its numerator/denominator behavior together.  
- Pole/zero view per lane with zeros always shown, including remote zeros.  
- Warnings: instability, near-degenerate rows, excessive gain, abrupt packed-middle discontinuity, Nyquist crowding, and sub-200 Hz collisions; sub-200 is warned, not banned.

**Constructor subset**

- `Dark Cliff`: descending foundation using lawful low/high attenuation structures and optional counterweight.  
- `Rising Edge`: shelf/edge foundation with controllable slope and air-cap behavior.  
- `Opposed Window`: paired hollow/peak behavior that can widen, cross, or stress over Morph
