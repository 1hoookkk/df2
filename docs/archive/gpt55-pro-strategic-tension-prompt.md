## The Product

- DF2 is a VST3 insert FX plugin.
- Public controls are `TYPE`, `MORPH`, and `SECONDARY`.
- `TYPE` selects one authored body. `MORPH` and `SECONDARY` perform within it.
- Commercial scope is only `4-7` original iconic bodies.
- The Forge is a private single-author tool and a recordable short-form-content
  surface. It is not shipped.
- The human author owns taste. The machine may propose lawful raw material and
  expose plots. It must not silently select, rank, bless, or assemble finished
  bodies.
- The author trusts plots first and ears last.

## Locked Runtime Truth

- A shipping body is exactly four corners:
  `M0_S0`, `M1_S0`, `M0_S1`, `M1_S1`.
- Each corner contains six serialized DF2T biquad rows.
- Each row stores five packed `u16` words.
- A body is exactly `4 x 6 x 5 x 2 = 240` bytes.
- Both numerator and denominator behavior matter. Never omit zeros.
- Runtime interpolation is packed-domain bilinear interpolation followed by
  decode and the serial cascade.
- The six rows are indexed correspondence lanes. They are not universally six
  semantic cavities, but they are not disposable bookkeeping either.

Do not reopen runtime format, sample rate, stage count, interpolation order, or
cube debates.

## What Morph Designer Actually Is

Keep the public Morph Designer tool separate from the sealed ROM-body
architecture.

Observed:

- Morph Designer is one compact class-specific writer targeting the same packed
  runtime banks.
- It exposes six serialized rows.
- Each row has a type plus low-frame frequency/gain and high-frame
  frequency/gain.
- Only three row grammars compile; disabled rows are skipped.
- Its strength is deliberate composition: stack rows, mix row grammars, and
  author a coherent `START -> END` journey.
- Historical Morph Designer authors two logical frames and duplicates them
  across Secondary. Its output surface is effectively one-dimensional:

```text
M0_S0 = M0_S1
M1_S0 = M1_S1
```

- Sealed ROM bodies are richer: their four corners are genuinely distinct.
- Other class-specific writers also target the same packed banks. Morph
  Designer is not a universal explanation for every sealed factory body.
