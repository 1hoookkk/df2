shipping reconstruction is a separate later step.

Every body is a byte-identical copy of a candidate in
`dev/tmp/iconic_presets_20260722/rom_frames_rich_sources_01/`.
At each of the four packed corners, S1 and S6 are complete rows from the named
ROM frame and S2 through S5 are complete rows from our rich-data body. Nothing
was decoded, refitted, sorted, normalized, repaired, or Q-derived during
promotion.

The complete packed six-stage product is the candidate. The S1/S6 quotient is
retained only as a diagnostic for unsafe gain and pathological shaping; it is
not a neutrality test or a claim that the two rows are universally portable.
Future work evaluates one deliberate frame-middle pairing at a time, with Tyson
making the musical keep/kill decision.
