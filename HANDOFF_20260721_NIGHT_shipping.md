## 5. DURABLE DSP LAWS proven in the prior filter session (belong in FILTER_NOTEBOOK.md)

These are load-bearing and currently live only in a temp scratchpad — capture them:
- **SCALE (=b0) is pure broadband level.** It scales a section's whole numerator, so it
  shifts a corner up/down uniformly and **cannot** change crown-vs-floor contrast. Use it
  ONLY to bound absolute crown (the +27 trim). The "floor-down/crown-keep" reshape is
  DEAD (measured).
- **A body = 4 independently authored corners** (M0_Q0, M100_Q0, M0_Q100, M100_Q100).
  **Q100 is a second authored pose, NEVER derived.** A "dead Q knob" means the Q100 poses
  were authored flat — fix = re-author just the two Q100 corners hot, leave the Q0 poses.
- **+27 dB corner-crown law** (ROM crowns sit −3..+27). Bound a hot body with a body-wide
  SCALE multiplier (pure vertical shift, morph relationships intact).
- **Contrast/character lives in poles+zeros.** Ear-SAFE lever = deepen **zeros** (carve
  valleys, poles untouched — restores crown-vs-floor without touching tuning). Ear-RISKY =
  Q-bloom pole surgery (the +0.010 uniform-radius boost was ear-killed).
- **Roster audit (141 bodies):** all stable/legal (max r 0.99955, 0 unstable/nonfinite),
  but **gain-budget wildly unbounded, 0→107 dB, median 43.** Bound with TWO numbers per
  corner: absolute crown (clip ceiling ≤+27) and contrast (character), not one.

  counterpoint / call-and-response / chords are how you *choreograph* those voices. That is
