NOTE RULER

A thin keyboard runs the floor. You place against notes you know — 808 ≈ E1, vocal body ≈ A3, snap ≈ A5. Best if you think in pitch. Handle shows note + Hz.
C

SOUND LANDMARKS

Faint flags mark where real material lives — KICK, VOX, SNAP, AIR. You place relative to the sound itself. Hz shows only on the handle you hold. Most intuitive, least "ruler."
