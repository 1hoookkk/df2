## What clicked

**Stages are consequences, not causes.** The whole gap of previous failed
authoring iterations was operating at stage level. Stages emerge from
source choice + deterministic fitter rules. Authoring is upstream.

Proven by: single Ee vowel fed into the forge produced Talking-Hedz-shape
composite with v0 sort-by-frequency rules, no stage-level intervention.

